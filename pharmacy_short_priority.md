Exactly. I think your instinct is right.

The poster shouldn't sell the **entire pharmacy operating system**. It should sell the **first obvious reason a pharmacy/clinic should want this**: _"People should be able to find us, get answers, see what we offer, and transact with us online."_

Then subtly show that there's more underneath.

## I would structure the poster like this

### **GTSS**

**Great Thumbs Software Solutions**

# **PUT YOUR PHARMACY ONLINE.**

### **Be found. Be available. Get business.**

**A powerful digital platform built for pharmacies & clinics in Nairobi.**

---

### 🔎 **BE FOUND ONLINE**

**Build a strong online presence that helps customers find your pharmacy when they're searching for healthcare services and medicines.**

**SEO • Google visibility • Professional website • Online presence**

---

### 💬 **ANSWER QUESTIONS — WITHOUT THE CALLS**

Give customers the information they need **24/7**.

**FAQs • Services & information • Customer care chatbot • Enquiries**

> _Reduce repetitive calls and let your website handle the common questions._

---

### 📅 **SHOW & MANAGE YOUR SERVICES**

Let customers see what you offer and **book services directly online.**

**Service listings • Online bookings • Booking management**

---

### 💊 **SELL OTC MEDICATION ONLINE**

Let customers browse your available OTC medicines and place orders directly through your website.

**Medicine catalogue • Online orders • Customer accounts**

---

### 💳 **GET PAID DIRECTLY**

Make checkout simple with **seamless M-Pesa payments.**

**M-Pesa • Online checkout • Payment tracking**

---

Then one very short transition:

## **AND THAT'S JUST THE START.**

### As your pharmacy grows, GTSS can help you digitize:

**Prescriptions • Refills • Inventory • Customer management • Loyalty • Medication adherence • Analytics**

---

### Bottom CTA

# **YOUR PHARMACY SHOULD BE ONLINE.**

**Let's build it.**

**GTSS — Great Thumbs Software Solutions**

**📞 Call / WhatsApp:** [Number]

### The key messaging hierarchy

I would make the visual emphasis:

**1. BE FOUND**
↓
**2. ANSWER CUSTOMERS**
↓
**3. GET BOOKINGS**
↓
**4. SELL OTC**
↓
**5. GET PAID**

That's a much better **entry-level pitch**.

You're essentially telling a pharmacy owner:

> **"First, let's make sure customers can find you and interact with you online. Then let's turn that online presence into actual business."**

And only after they're interested do you introduce the harder operational capabilities like prescriptions, inventory, refills, adherence, CRM, analytics, etc.

This also aligns well with the actual platform: it already has the public medicine catalogue, service booking, guest orders, refill/order forms, AI chatbot, enquiries, and M-Pesa infrastructure.

**I would NOT put things like FEFO, inventory lots, AI medication import, caregiver permissions, etc. on this poster.** Those are excellent _sales/demo material_, but terrible _first-impression poster material_.
