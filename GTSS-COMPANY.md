## Company Overview

**Great Thumbs Software Solutions (GTSS)** is a parent software company that designs, builds, manages, and distributes practical software solutions that empower local businesses, institutions, and communities through technology.

### Positioning Statement

> *"GTSS builds practical, scalable software products for real-world local problems."*

### Business Structure

GTSS operates as a **parent company** with a portfolio of:

- **Products** – Sellable software systems (SaaS & one-time solutions)
- **Platforms** – Community or marketplace ecosystems

Each product/platform operates with its own branding, landing page, and pricing model, all managed centrally by GTSS.
