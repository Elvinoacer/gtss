### 1️⃣ Primary Color (Identity & Authority)

**Deep Navy Blue**
`#0F172A`

**Why this is strong**

- Conveys trust, stability, engineering
- Works for SaaS, B2B, enterprise
- Scales well as GTSS grows
- Neutral enough to host many sub-brands

Use for:

- Top navigation
- Sidebar background
- Logo text
- Primary buttons (default state)

---

### 2️⃣ Secondary Color (Energy & Action)

**Royal Blue / Indigo**
`#2563EB`

**Why**

- Feels modern and tech-forward
- Strong contrast against dark navy
- Perfect for CTAs and links

Use for:

- Call-to-action buttons
- Links
- Active menu items
- Focus states

---

### 3️⃣ Accent Color (Growth & Success)

**Emerald Green**
`#10B981`

**Why**

- Signals success, confirmation, growth
- Not aggressive like red
- Works well in dashboards and analytics

Use for:

- Success states
- Completed tasks
- Active subscriptions
- Positive metrics

---

### 4️⃣ Warning / Attention Color

**Amber / Warm Orange**
`#F59E0B`

**Why**

- Grabs attention without panic
- Professional warning tone

Use for:

- Pending actions
- Warnings
- Important notices

---

### 5️⃣ Error Color (Clear but Calm)

**Soft Red**
`#EF4444`

Use for:

- Errors
- Failed actions
- Critical alerts only

---

## 🧩 Neutral Colors (Very Important)

### Backgrounds

- **Primary background:** `#F8FAFC` (light, clean)
- **Secondary background:** `#E5E7EB` (cards, dividers)

### Text

- **Primary text:** `#111827`
- **Secondary text:** `#6B7280`

These keep the UI readable and professional.

---

## 🖼️ Dark Mode (Optional but recommended)

If you add dark mode later:

- Background: `#020617`
- Cards: `#0F172A`
- Text: `#E5E7EB`
- Accent blue still works perfectly

---

## 🎯 Color Usage Rule (this matters)

Follow this rule strictly:

- **60%** neutral colors
- **30%** primary (navy)
- **10%** accent (blue/green/orange/red combined)

This prevents the UI from looking noisy or childish.

---

## 🧠 Why this theme fits YOUR situation

- GTSS is a **parent company** → needs authority
- You manage **multiple products** → needs neutrality
- You’ll have **dashboards + sales + docs** → needs clarity
- You want **long-term credibility** → not trendy colors

This palette won’t age quickly.
