# Great Thumbs Software Solutions (GTSS)

## 1. Company Overview

**Company Name:** Great Thumbs Software Solutions (GTSS)

**Nature:** Parent software company

**Core Purpose:**
GTSS exists to design, build, manage, and distribute practical software solutions that empower local businesses, institutions, and communities through technology.

**Positioning Statement:**

> "GTSS builds practical, scalable software products for real-world local problems."

GTSS acts as the **central engine** that owns and operates:

- Software products (SaaS & one-time solutions)
- Platforms (community or marketplace-based)
- Infrastructure (cloud, databases, services)
- Teams and operations

---

## 2. Business Structure

### 2.1 Parent–Child Model

GTSS is the **parent company**.

Under GTSS:

- **Products:** Sellable software systems
- **Platforms:** Community or marketplace ecosystems (e.g. JustInTime)

Each product/platform:

- Has its own branding
- Has its own landing page
- Has its own pricing model
- Is managed centrally by GTSS

---

## 3. Product Categories

GTSS supports multiple product monetization models:

### 3.1 One-Time Payment Products

- Offline Hotel Ordering System
- Custom enterprise installations

### 3.2 Subscription-Based Products

- Single-store E-commerce system
- Church Management System

### 3.3 Platform Products

- JustInTime (Community service access)
- Multi-vendor E-commerce platform

---

## 4. Product Lifecycle Management

Each product inside GTSS follows the same lifecycle:

1. Product creation
2. Documentation & instructions
3. Pricing definition
4. Demo & media attachment
5. Sales enablement (cards, QR, scripts)
6. Deployment & onboarding
7. Maintenance & updates

---

## 6. Team & Member Management

GTSS includes a **Member Management System**.

### 6.1 Member Roles

#### Founder / Director

- Full system access
- Product approval
- Pricing control

#### Admin

- Manage members
- Manage products
- Assign roles

#### Product Manager

- Manage assigned products
- Update documentation
- Track product performance

#### Sales Representative

- Access assigned products
- Generate referral links
- Track commissions

#### Support Staff

- Access customer tickets
- Product usage troubleshooting

---

### 6.2 Role Permissions

Each role has controlled permissions for:

- Viewing products
- Editing documentation
- Generating QR codes
- Accessing sales materials
- Viewing analytics

---

## 7. Sales & Distribution System

### 7.1 Sales Workflow

1. Product assigned to sales rep
2. QR code / referral link generated
3. Client scans QR or contacts GTSS
4. Demo scheduled or instant access
5. Sale completed
6. Commission recorded

---

### 7.2 Commission Models

- One-time products: Fixed or percentage-based
- Subscription products: First-month commission
- Enterprise products: Negotiated finder’s fee
