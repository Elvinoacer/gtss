 1\. Identity Section  
  Located in Step 1  
   \- Product Name (Required): The display name of your product.  
   \- Category (Required): Select from SaaS, Enterprise, Mobile App, or Platform.  
   \- Marketing Tagline: A short, compelling phrase to appear as a quote.  
   \- Website URL: Link to the product's landing page(leave this i will fill later).  
   \- Short Description: A brief summary of what the product does (supports Markdown).  
   \- Search Tags: Keywords to help find the product.  
   \- Product Logo: Upload an image file for the product icon(i will do this manually).

  2\. Description & Details Section  
  Located in Step 2 (This step combines details, features, assets, and reviews)  
   \- Detailed Instructions: Step-by-step installation and usage guide (supports Markdown).  
   \- Key Features: A dynamic list where you can add multiple features. For each feature:  
     \- Feature Title  
     \- Description  
   \- Health Metrics: KPIs to display on the dashboard. For each metric:  
     \- Label (e.g., Uptime)  
     \- Value (e.g., 99.9%)  
     \- Percentage (0-100 for the progress bar)  
   \- Product Assets(i will do this manually, leave it): Downloadable files or links. For each asset:  
     \- Asset Title  
     \- Type (Select: PDF Document, Video, Slides, External Link)  
     \- URL / Link  
   \- Technical Architecture: details about the tech stack (supports Markdown).  
   \- Customer Reviews: Testimonials to display. For each review:  
     \- Author Name (Required)  
     \- Role / Position  
     \- Avatar URL  
     \- Rating (1-5 Stars)  
     \- Review Content (Required)

  3\. Pricing Section  
  Located in Step 3 (Labeled as "Features" in the wizard progress bar)  
   \- Pricing Model: Select one of the following:  
     \- One-Time Payment: Single fee for lifetime access.  
     \- Subscription: Recurring billing.  
     \- Free / Open Source: No cost.  
   \- Price (USD): Cost in US Dollars (required if not Free).  
   \- Price (KES): Cost in Kenyan Shillings (required if not Free).  
   \- Billing Cycle: Select Monthly, Yearly, or Quarterly (only visible for Subscription model).

  4\. Media Section  
  Located in Step 4 (Labeled as "Pricing" in the wizard progress bar)  
   \- YouTube Video URL: Full URL for the product demo video.  
   \- Product Gallery: Upload up to 5 high-quality screenshots or marketing images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, max 2MB each).
